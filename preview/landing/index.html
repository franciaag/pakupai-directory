Why these pages, and the evidence behind them

Recommend 1, "Your next idea. The right AI help." It leads with the work a visitor wants to do, then offers a clear path to finding help. It does not promise that an agent will finish the work. Option 2 gives the whole product a more visual, interactive menu. Both replace product-first vocabulary with task-first benefits.

These are design hypotheses, not proven conversion gains. No tracking, fake testimonials, customer counts, private account activity or universal compatibility claims. Real directory data and the current Room setup guide ground the feature claims.

Thin rules, compact controls, capped width and grayscale match the product. Filled buttons signal the primary next step. Rounded containers are limited to product evidence previews, not decorative feature cards. No motion or oversized empty space delays the content.

Test whether visitors can explain the product after the first screen. Measure public discovery/comparison and verified Room setup separately. A click or signup is not proof of a working agent connection.
